`> whoami`

I'm **Fabian / Noxi** and I'm working towards a career in penetration testing. 

I've always been fascinated by hackers, and I still think what thy do is incredibly exciting. I saw a YouTube video in 2024 in which a red teamer talked about breaking into banks, and how cool it is that those very banks pay him to do it. 
I remembered trying to find out about this when I was 16 or so, and I had no luck finding sites like Hack The Box, TCM Security or TryHackMe. I decided to try again, but this time I found a surprising amount of information about how to become a pentester, I wasn't very happy with the jobs I'd had so far and wanted to do something I really enjoy and started to learn about the possible positions and how to get hands on a Cyber Securrity job. 

I started with no prior knowledge, so I had to begin at the very beginning. When I tried to find out how to actually learn this, I stumbled upon countless websites and videos claiming to teach me how to become a pentester. 
It took me a long time, and I could barely solve an easy-rated CTF. I even quit for a few months because I thought I couldn't make it. I think the biggest motivation that brought me back is the chance to learn new things, get better, and help making systems more secure. 

What drives me today is beating CTFs and understanding how systems fail and most importantly having fun and always learning! I'm fascinated of the idea that what ever technologie you find no matter if hardware, IoT or software can be used in unintended ways like breaking a logic on a website or reverse engeneering software and many more.
